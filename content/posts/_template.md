the URL: `/writing/building-the-first-scene`.

## Write in your own voice

Plain markdown works: `##` for section headings, `*italic*`, `**bold**`,
`> ` for a pull quote, and `-` for bullets.

## What belongs here

Pieces only you can write — the specific ones, not the general ones:

- How a particular scene, chapter, or shot came together, and what changed
- A decision you made in production and the reasoning behind it
- What went wrong on a project and what you did about it
- Excerpts from the work with commentary on the craft behind them
- Why a film or book influenced how you approach a specific problem

Avoid anything that reads like it could have come from any other site.
A post titled "10 Tips For Screenwriting" is worth nothing here. A post
about the argument you had with yourself over cutting a character is
worth everything — nobody else can write it.
